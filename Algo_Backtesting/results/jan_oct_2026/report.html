    How to read this report

    Profit factor: total money won divided by total money lost; above 1 means profitable. Win rate: share of trades that made money; a low win rate can still work if wins are bigger than losses. Max drawdown: the largest fall from a peak. Sharpe: return per unit of day-to-day swings; above 1 is decent. Capital used for percentages is the busiest-day exposure (max trades x amount per trade). Costs include brokerage, STT, exchange, GST, stamp duty, SEBI fees and slippage. Fills are assumed at the candle prices shown; very thin stocks would fill worse in real trading, and prices are not adjusted for splits or bonuses.
