Iska matlab

"Median galti" = aadhe points me model ka price asli se isse kam alag tha. ITM option (jo hum sell karte hain) pe galti kam hai, kyunki uska zyada hissa intrinsic value hai. "Median jhukaav" minus = model asli se thoda sasta, plus = thoda mehenga. 5 saal ke liye ek hi k lagaya hai, jabki asli IV har din badalta hai.
