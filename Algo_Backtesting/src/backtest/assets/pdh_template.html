Faisla (seedhi baat)

    Fit aur verify

    In-sample = setting chunne wala hissa. Out-of-sample = baad ka hissa, jahan setting badli nahi. Stress = slippage dugna (2 point). "Raw R" = bina slippage aur kharche ke.

    Equity curve (kul profit)

    Har trade ke baad kul net profit (Rs), kharcha aur slippage kaat ke. Dotted line = out-of-sample shuru.

    Drawdown

    Sabse upar wale point se kitna neeche (Rs).

    R multiple distribution

    Har trade ka net R (kharche ke baad). -1R = pura SL. Scratch = -0.1R se +0.1R.

    Trade kaise band hue

    Saare numbers

    Signal kyun chhode

    Signal aaya par trade nahi hua - kis wajah se (poora samay).

    Paisa kaise lagaya (investment)

    Return % hamesha shuruaati capital pe hai. Profit dobara invest nahi kiya (compounding nahi).

    (a) "Discipline se 60-65% win rate"

    Sabse zyada win rate wali 10 settings (kam se kam 100 trade, poora samay). Win = net R > +0.1, loss = net R < -0.1, baaki scratch.

    (b) "PDH / PDL baaki levels se behtar hai"

    Upar chuni setting ke saare rules wahi, sirf level badla. Random = har din kisi doosre din ki PDH / PDL doori pe level, 20 baar (average, aur sabse kam - sabse zyada).

    (c) "11:00 ke baad trade 9:30 se behtar"

    Saari settings ka average. "Jodi" = do settings jo sirf time window me alag hain.

    (d) "1R pe aadha book + breakeven SL behtar"

    Har exit ki saari settings ka average. Aakhri 4 column: kitne % jodon me ye exit F1 / F2 (bina partial) se behtar tha (average net R).
