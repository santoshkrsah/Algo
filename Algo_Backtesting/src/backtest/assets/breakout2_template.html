Overfitting ki chetavani

"High-volume breakouts ~80% success" - asli me kitna?

Har breakout (mahine ki limit ke bina, volume 1.5x ya zyada, baaki filters main test jaise). Fail = 5 din me koi close base top ke neeche (wapas base ke andar). +1R / +2R = stop (base top - 0.5%) se pehle utna upar gaya (20 din tak). A jeeta = type A trade (kharche ke baad) profit me.

Nifty regime ke hisaab se breakouts

"4-6% mahina = 18 mahine me capital double" - hamare result se?

Mahina return = us mahine ka mark-to-market net / capital. Double ke liye 18 mahine me ~3.9% har mahina compounding chahiye (bina compounding 5.6%).
